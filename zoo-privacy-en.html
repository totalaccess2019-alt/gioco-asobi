1 Play information and settings

The app stores game results, dates and times, settings, zoo and animal growth, achievements, and similar information on your device. It uses this information to resume play, display results, and reflect growth. Games that use photos also store start and end dates and times, the number of questions, counts of correct and incorrect answers, skips and exclusions, and question-selection conditions. The app has no feature that automatically sends these records to the operator’s servers.

2 Access to photos

In games that use photos, the app reads the parts of your photo library you have permitted it to access and uses them for questions and display. Games that ask about the order of photos compare the dates and times recorded in the photo library. These may differ from when the photos were actually taken.

In the current version, photos and thumbnails for display, and candidates for questions, are handled temporarily in memory. Photo-game results do not record the photos themselves, their identifiers, or the date and time of each photo. However, to remember photos excluded from questions, the app saves their identifiers on your device. The app does not modify or delete the original photos in your photo library.

You can change or revoke photo access in iOS Settings. If you choose limited access, only the photos you have permitted are included. Revoking permission stops new reads of photos, but does not automatically erase past results or exclusion information.

3 Purchase information

The app uses Apple’s App Store and StoreKit for in-app purchases, restoring purchases, and checking purchase entitlements. It uses entitlement information provided by Apple to unlock and restore purchased features. The operator does not independently collect or store credit card numbers or Apple Account credentials through the app.

4 Advertising and tracking

The app does not serve advertisements or track activity across other companies’ apps and services. It also has no feature that sends play information to an analytics service used by the app.

5 Retention and deletion

Play information, settings, and exclusion information remain on your device until you use the corresponding deletion operation or delete the app. Deleting a photo game’s history resets the relevant history and the zoo’s growth. This operation alone does not delete original photos, identifiers of excluded photos, stars, badges, purchase entitlements, or settings. You can clear exclusions using “外した写真を戻す” (restore excluded photos) in each photo game.

To let you undo history deletion, the app keeps a copy of the affected history and zoo growth for seven days. After that period, the copy is deleted the next time you launch the app or perform a related operation. It is not automatically erased at the exact time the period expires while the app remains unopened. The date, time and status of the deletion operation, and administrative information used to prevent results from being counted again, remain on the device afterward. This administrative information has no automatic deletion deadline and does not include the photos themselves.

If copies of photos were saved within the app by an older version, they are deleted when you open the “自分の写真” (my photos) screen. Updating the app alone does not necessarily delete them immediately.

6 Apple services and backups

Retrieving photos stored in iCloud Photos, or making or restoring purchases, may involve communication with Apple’s services. Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles information.

Apple Privacy Policy

Some storage areas are excluded from operating-system backups, but ordinary brain-training history, settings, and other information may be included. Deleting information within the app does not also delete existing operating-system backups, original photos, or Apple’s purchase records. To delete the app’s data from your device, use “Delete App.” “Offload App” leaves data on the device. Please manage backups separately.

7 Contacting us

Contacting us is optional. We use the email address, message, and attachments received with an inquiry to respond and investigate the issue. We use Gmail to receive inquiries. We retain this information for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any follow-up, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

We receive photos and play history only when you send them as needed. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information. For inquiries, questions about information handling, or requests to delete information the operator has received, contact giocoasobi@gmail.com.

Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for how Google, the provider of Gmail, handles information.
