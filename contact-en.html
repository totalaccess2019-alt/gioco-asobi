Selecting the link opens your email app. If it does not open, copy the address above into your email app.

When contacting us

Please include the app’s name and your question. For a problem with the app, the app version, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue can help us investigate.

Please send photos, play history, backups, or similar files only when needed. Avoid including unnecessary personal information.

How we handle inquiries

Contacting us is optional. We use the email address, message, and attachments received with an inquiry to respond and investigate the issue. We use Gmail to receive inquiries. We retain this information for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any follow-up, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for how Google, the provider of Gmail, handles information.
