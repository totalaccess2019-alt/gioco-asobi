1 Play information and settings

The app stores the current puzzle board, notes, progress, completion records, observation notes, favorites, garden state, settings, and similar information on your device. It uses this information to resume play and display records. The game screens run using programs included in the app. The app has no feature that automatically sends play information to the operator’s servers.

2 Purchase information

The app uses Apple’s App Store and StoreKit to purchase paid features, restore purchases, and check purchase entitlements. It uses entitlement information provided by Apple to unlock and restore purchased features. The prices shown on the purchase screen are obtained from Apple.

The operator does not independently collect or store credit card numbers or Apple Account credentials through the app. Purchase and restoration processes involve communication with Apple. Apple’s purchase records are managed separately from the app’s play records.

3 Exporting and importing backups

When you export a backup, the app creates an unencrypted JSON file containing play information and settings. You choose where to save or share it. If you choose cloud storage or another sharing destination, that service receives the file. Please check how the destination handles the file and avoid sharing it with anyone who does not need it.

When you import a backup, the app processes the file you select on your device to restore play information. The app’s backup feature does not automatically send the file to the operator’s servers.

4 Advertising and tracking

The app does not serve advertisements or track activity across other companies’ apps and services. It also has no feature that sends play information to an analytics service used by the app.

5 Retention and deletion

Play information and settings have no automatic retention limit. “進捗を初期化” (reset progress) on the settings screen resets progress, collection items, generated puzzles, and tutorial state, but retains settings. Restarting a puzzle affects only that puzzle. These operations do not erase purchase entitlements or Apple’s purchase records.

When you export a backup, the app also creates a file in its temporary storage. The app attempts to delete temporary files when sharing ends and in other cleanup situations. Files may remain if the process is interrupted or deletion fails. The app also attempts to delete any remaining temporary files during the first export after the app next launches. Please manage files saved or shared to other locations separately.

To delete the app’s data from your device, use “Delete App.” “Offload App” leaves data on the device. Deleting the app alone does not delete exported files or existing operating-system backups. The app’s data may be included in operating-system backups. Delete unwanted files at their saved locations, and manage backups separately through your device settings or the settings of the services you use.

6 Apple services and external links

Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy for how information is handled by Apple’s services. If you open an external link from the app, such as a reference resource, that website’s privacy policy applies.

Apple Privacy Policy

7 Contacting us

Contacting us is optional. We use the email address, message, and attachments received with an inquiry to respond and investigate the issue. We use Gmail to receive inquiries. We retain this information for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any follow-up, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

We receive play records and backups only when you send them as needed. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information. For inquiries, questions about information handling, or requests to delete information the operator has received, contact giocoasobi@gmail.com.

Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for how Google, the provider of Gmail, handles information.

Google Privacy Policy

8 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the revised policy and its last updated date on this page.
